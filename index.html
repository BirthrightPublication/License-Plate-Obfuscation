About classifications & risk

Legal: reviewed permission within a stated scope. Illegal: reviewed prohibition. Obscure: conflicting or ambiguous interpretation. Unverified: evidence not reviewed or unavailable.

Risk is separate: 1 Minimal, 2 Low, 3 Moderate, 4 High, 5 Critical. No level is assigned without evidence; missing data never means low risk.
