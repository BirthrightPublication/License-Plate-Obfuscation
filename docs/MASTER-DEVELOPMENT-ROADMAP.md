## V0.4 — LEGAL INTELLIGENCE

Implement four legal classifications:

- Legal: Satisfies all applicable requirements that have actually been verified.
- Illegal: Violates an applicable, verified requirement.
- Obscure: Genuine ambiguity or conflicting interpretation exists.
- Unverified: Insufficient current authoritative information exists.

Implement a five-level Legal Threat Level indicator for regulatory compliance risk.

Include:

- State-specific laws
- Official source citations
- Effective dates
- Registration classification
- Plate count requirements
- Mounting requirements
- Frame and cover restrictions
- Accessory restrictions
- Lighting requirements
- Legal rule version history
- Explanation of each determination

Never equate software classification with guaranteed legal approval.
